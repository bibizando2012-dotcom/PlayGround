The one rule that changes everything

Capture. Swap. Survive.

01

Move like chess

All the familiar moves are here — castling, en passant and promotion included. Tap a piece, then a highlighted square.

02

Every capture is a swap

The survivor takes the fallen piece's movement power. A pawn that takes a queen becomes a queen. A queen that takes a pawn becomes a pawn — unless that pawn would be stranded on the far rank, where it must promote instead.

03

The crown stays royal

A king keeps its crown even when its movement changes. It can become a royal knight, rook, bishop or pawn — but it is still the piece you must protect.

04

Win by checkmate

There is no capturing the king. Trap the enemy crown with no legal escape and the match is yours. Stalemate and repetition are draws.
