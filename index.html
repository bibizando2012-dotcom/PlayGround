About the hollow

Nothing to do.
Everything to notice.

There is no quest here, no counter, and no ending. Wander the long grass, watch the light move, or simply sit with the wind.

On touch

  • Left thumb — drift through the meadow
  • Right drag — look around
  • Pinch — move the camera in or out
  • Hop — a small, joyful lift

On keyboard

  • W A S D — wander
  • Space — hop
  • Mouse drag — look
  • Scroll — zoom

Tilt to steer

Use your device's motion sensors as a gentle steering assist. On iPhone and iPad, permission is requested only when you switch it on.
