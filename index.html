Field guide

How to do nothing

01
Wander

Use the little rose of arrows, or WASD. There is no destination.

02
Look

Drag anywhere on the meadow. Pinch to come closer. Let go and the hare keeps breathing.

03
Hop

Tap the pale button, or press space. The ears lag behind. This is correct.

04
Say hello

Tap the hare. It may lean into your hand. It may simply twitch an ear.

05
Tilt

Optional. Lean the device to steer. On iPhone or iPad, iOS asks for motion access the first time you switch it on.

06
Turn the light

The sun sits low in the sky. Tap the sun to trade golden hour for moonrise; the fireflies come out.

No objectives. No inventory. No wrong way to be a hare.
